BandMayhem – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 August 2026 · Last updated: 22 August 2026

This policy explains how EnragedTomatoStudios (“we”, “us”) processes information when you use the BandMayhem mobile game (“BandMayhem” or the “App”). Contact: bandmayhem.app@gmail.com.

1. Information we process

  • Account data: a Firebase user identifier is created so the App can operate. If you link Google or Apple sign-in, we may also receive your email address, display name and provider identifier.
  • Game and user-generated data: career progress, cloud saves and backups, manager and band names, choices, achievements, leaderboard/Hall of Fame submissions, public career rooms, quiz challenges, reactions and other game content.
  • Generated media: fictional band images, band photos and album covers created or stored through App features, together with related prompts and game context.
  • Purchases: product, store, purchase time, entitlement status, price/currency when available, and hashed or stable transaction references used to validate delivery and prevent duplicate redemption. We do not receive full payment-card details.
  • Analytics and diagnostics: app interactions, screens, sessions, lifecycle events, purchase events, crash logs, stack traces, device/app information, operating-system version, language, country/region, app-instance or installation identifiers and approximate location inferred from IP address.
  • Advertising data: Google Mobile Ads may process IP address, approximate location, device or advertising identifiers, ad impressions/clicks, user product interactions and diagnostics. BandMayhem requests non-personalized ads, but this technical processing still occurs.
  • Notifications and security: push-notification token and app version, IP address, user agent and hashed identifiers used for authentication, rate limiting, abuse prevention and fraud prevention.

2. Why we use this information

We use it to provide and synchronize the game, authenticate users, restore purchases, generate requested game content, operate leaderboards and community features, send requested notifications, measure and improve performance, diagnose crashes, show and measure advertising, protect users and services, prevent fraud and comply with legal obligations.

3. Service providers and disclosures

We do not sell personal information. Information is processed by service providers only as needed for the purposes above:

  • Google Firebase: Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions, Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config and Cloud Messaging.
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): serving, measuring and protecting advertising. AdMob may collect and share advertising-related data with Google and its advertising partners.
  • OpenAI: processing prompts, fictional character images and game context when an AI image or editorial feature is requested.
  • Google Play and Apple App Store: purchase processing and verification.

We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect the rights, safety and integrity of BandMayhem, its users or others.

4. Retention

Account and game data is retained while the account is active or as needed to provide the App. Diagnostics and analytics are retained according to our configured provider settings. Public game content remains until it is removed or the related account is deleted. When an account is deleted, directly linked account and game data is deleted; limited transaction hashes, product/store details and security records may be retained without the raw account identifier where necessary to prevent transaction reuse, investigate fraud, meet accounting obligations or comply with law.

5. Account and data deletion

Delete inside BandMayhem: open Settings → Privacy → Delete Account and confirm. This permanently deletes the Firebase authentication account and associated cloud data, including saves, profile, generated media, careers, Hall of Fame/leaderboard entries, public career rooms, notifications, diagnostics, entitlements and pending game rewards.

Request deletion on the web: email bandmayhem.app@gmail.com with the subject “BandMayhem Account Deletion”. State the sign-in provider and email used for the account. We may request reasonable verification. We aim to complete verified requests within 30 days.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Data already aggregated or de-identified so it no longer identifies the account may remain. Provider-controlled analytics or advertising data is also subject to the provider’s deletion and retention controls.

6. Your choices and rights

You may avoid linking Google or Apple sign-in, decline optional notification permissions, use device advertising/privacy controls and delete your account. Depending on your location, you may also have rights to access, correct, restrict, object to, port or erase personal data. Send requests to bandmayhem.app@gmail.com.

7. Children

BandMayhem is not directed to children under 13, or the minimum age required in their country to consent to data processing. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children below that age. Contact us if you believe this has occurred.

8. Security and international processing

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, including encrypted network transport. Service providers may process information in countries other than your own, subject to their applicable safeguards and contractual protections. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

9. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as BandMayhem changes. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are published. Questions or privacy requests: bandmayhem.app@gmail.com.
